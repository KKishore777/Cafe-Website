Featured Special
Serving up Something for Everyone.

From bold brews to sweet sips, every drink at The Tavern is crafted with care and community in mind. Whether you’re grabbing your go-to latte or trying something new like our signature Humble Mornings Blend, or delicious pastries from Hunny Bunny Bakery, there’s something on the menu to make everyone feel at home.

Grab a Bag of our Best Brew!

Take a piece of The Tavern home with you. Every 12oz bag of our signature roast fuels more than your morning — it powers job training, mentorship, and hope right here in Buckeye. Brew boldly. Give generously.

Baked Goods, Pastries, & Sandwiches

Better Together: The Tavern × Hunny Bunny’s Bakery

Every pastry we serve is handcrafted by Hunny Bunny Bakery, a local gem we’re proud to support. It’s a sweet partnership that supports local business and builds up Buckeye—one bite at a time. It’s more than a treat—it’s a taste of Buckeye’s small business spirit, made fresh just for you.

Repurposed. Reimagined. Reigniting Buckeye—one cup at a time.

The Tavern Coffee House is a nonprofit café where strong coffee fuels second chances. We’re creating jobs, building community, and serving hope daily in the heart of Buckeye.
